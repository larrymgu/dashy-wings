● Control the red triangle ship with your mouse. It faces the cursor and stays
centered on screen.
● Move the cursor farther away to increase speed or closer to slow down.
● Left-click to dash forward. The ship turns blue during its 1.5-second cooldown;
you can dash again when it turns red.
● Rectangular image enemies follow the ship. They spawn every three seconds
within X and Y coordinates of −15 to 15, at least three units from the ship.
● Hit an enemy during the actual dash movement to destroy it and earn one point.
Each point plays a sound and updates the top-right score.
● Blue cooldown color does not provide protection after the dash movement ends.
● Gray rectangles are stationary obstacles and walls. Touching one kills you, even
while dashing.
● Touching an enemy while not dashing also kills you.
● Reach five points to win. The game stops and displays “You Win!” or “Game
Over,” along with your final score
